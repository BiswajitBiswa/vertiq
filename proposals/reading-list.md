# Reading list

References for future work. Saved for review; not yet assessed against Vertiq's design.

## Recursive self-improvement

| Resource | What it is | Relevance to Vertiq |
|---|---|---|
| [Sakana AI: RSI Lab](https://sakana.ai/rsi-lab/) | Announcement of Sakana AI's research group on recursive self-improvement: AI systems that improve their own architectures and conduct research, using compute-efficient methods rather than scale, with safeguards in the improvement loop and open publication of results, including negative ones. | Comparable aims to Vertiq's RSI loop. Worth reviewing for how they bound self-improvement and report failed iterations. |
| [awesome-rsi](https://github.com/lobehub/awesome-rsi) | A community-maintained, CC0-licensed list from the LobeHub organisation, collecting papers, frameworks, benchmarks, posts and talks on recursive self-improvement. Organised by topic, including model-level training, harness and memory evolution, multi-agent systems, automated AI research, evolutionary search and safety. | A source of prior work and benchmarks for positioning the evaluation-gated loop. A candidate list to submit Vertiq to once public-data results exist. |

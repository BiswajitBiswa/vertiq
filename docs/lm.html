Vertiq LM

Vertiq LM

Vertiq LM maps an unfamiliar table to Vertiq's data contract. It identifies the entity, timestamp, event type, value and grouping columns, the attributes to retain and the date format. It is Qwen 3.5 4B (Apache 2.0) fine-tuned with LoRA on an Apple M1 Pro with 16 GB of memory, and it is evaluated on tables from domains excluded from training.

Findings

Adding column types, inferred from the values, to the prompt raised exact matches on unseen domains from 14.6% to 31.2%. Inferring date formats and value types from the rows improved every model.

Preference round

ORPO on the round 3 model's own mistakes raised exact matches after validation from 44.7% to 53.5%, and on unseen domains from 33.3% to 41.7%. To fit 16 GB, the 8% of preference examples longer than 460 tokens were left out. DPO was not used because it keeps a second copy of the model. Constrained decoding was evaluated and not adopted: it would correct 2 of 88 remaining errors.

Reproduction

lm/make_data.py, lm/train.sh, lm/evaluate.py, lm/collect_failures.py and lm/pref.sh; lm/round3.sh runs the production-like round end to end.

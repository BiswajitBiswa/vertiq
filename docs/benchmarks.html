Benchmark results

Each scenario uses synthetic data with a known ground truth, so results can be checked against the correct answer. Values are means ± standard deviation over 5 runs. The full suite runs in 196 seconds on a laptop using 4 cores (arm64, Python 3.10.10).

Real data: Online Retail II

Repeat purchase on the UCI Online Retail II dataset (CC BY 4.0): 1,067,371 invoice lines from a UK online retailer, 2009 to 2011. Cancellations and lines without a customer are removed, and lines are combined into 36,975 orders from 5,881 customers. The task predicts whether a customer orders again within 60 days, with point-in-time features and 20% of customers held out. The baseline is the recency rule. Recency baseline AUC: 0.761.

One dataset and one task. The scikit-learn row uses the same features and split and confirms the linear learner's implementation. Boosted trees score slightly higher; the automatic choice, made by 3-fold cross-validation on the training customers, picked BoostedClassifier. Reproduce with python benchmarks/public/online_retail.py --data online_retail_II.xlsx.

Scope. The synthetic benchmarks verify that the methods detect real signal, reject noise and do not promote weaker models; the Online Retail II result shows the same pipeline on real transactions. Neither measures accuracy on a specific organisation's data; the evaluation gate measures that on held-out records at every training run. Further public benchmarks (the Hillstrom email uplift dataset and NASA C-MAPSS) are planned.
